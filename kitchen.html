🌡️ Measure & Temp

Potica is done at ~90 °C inside, and yeast dies above ~45 °C — numbers beat vibes.

Digital kitchen scale (grams)
The single most important tool on this page. 500 g of flour is not "about 4 cups."
Economy →
Instant-read thermometer
Milk at 37 °C, loaf at 90 °C — ends both arguments instantly.
Economy →
Oven thermometer
Most home ovens lie by 10–20 degrees. A cracked top is often just a hot oven.
Economy →
Measuring spoons (narrow)
For the yeast, salt, cinnamon, and clove — narrow ones fit spice jars.
Economy →
Measuring cups (liquid)
Warm milk by the millilitre.
Economy →
Folding bread proofer
A warm, humidity-controlled box — the Iron Range grandmothers heated the whole house; this heats one cubic foot.
