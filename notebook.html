Only five fillings are legally 'Slovenska potica'

Under EU Regulation 2021/656, a potica sold as protected "Slovenska potica" may contain exactly five fillings: walnut, walnut with raisins, poppy seed, cottage cheese, or tarragon. Everything else is delicious — but it isn't the protected article. The full specification →
