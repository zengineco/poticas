How Do You Pronounce Potica?

By Poticas.com  ·  Updated July 2026

Three syllables, stress in the middle, and the c is a "ts." Say it once correctly and a Slovenian grandmother somewhere smiles.

How do you pronounce potica?

Potica is pronounced poh-TEET-sah — three syllables, stress in the middle, with the c sounding like "ts." It is not "poh-TIK-uh." The Croatian form povitica is poh-vee-TEET-sah, four syllables with the same stressed "TEET."

The Short Answer

Poh-TEET-sah. Three syllables. The stress lands on the middle syllable, and the final -ca is pronounced "tsah," never "kah." Said at speed it flows — poteetsa — the way you'd say it reaching across a holiday table.

The plural is potice (poh-TEET-seh) in Slovene, though English speakers saying "poticas" are in good company — it is the name of this website, after all.

Why the C Sounds Like TS

In Slovene (and across most Slavic Latin alphabets), the letter c is always pronounced "ts" — like the end of "cats." It is never a hard "k" and never a soft "s." Once you know that one rule, half the pastry's family tree pronounces itself: potica, povitica, Vodnik's Kuharske bukve, the potičnik pan. The related letter č (with the little hook) is "ch," as in "church."

Common Mispronunciations

  • "poh-TIK-uh" — the most common miss: reading the c as a k. It's "ts."
  • "POH-tih-kah" — stress on the first syllable. The middle syllable carries it.
  • "poh-TEE-kah" — halfway there; the vowel is right but the c still needs its "ts."

And in Kansas City, where the pastry is povitica, the local bakery spells the sound out for customers as "Poh-Va-Teet-Sa" — same music, one extra beat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it pronounced potika or poteetsa?

Poteetsa. In Slovene the letter c is always pronounced "ts" (as in "cats"), so potica is poh-TEET-sah. "Poh-TIK-uh" is the most common mispronunciation.

How do you pronounce povitica?

Poh-vee-TEET-sah — four syllables, stress on "TEET." Kansas City's Strawberry Hill bakery renders it for customers as "Poh-Va-Teet-Sa."

What is the plural of potica?

In Slovene, potice (poh-TEET-seh). In casual English usage, "poticas" is common and universally understood.
